## Product — FOCUS (the only product on sale) — LAB-CONFIRMED FORMULA (source of truth)

Confirmed by the manufacturing lab (Nov 2026). This overrides the website, which still describes
chewable "pastilles" with 80 mg caffeine and a fruity taste — those are OUTDATED, never use them.

- Format: **gélules végétales** (vegan capsules, hypromellose shell), **flacon de 30 gélules**
  (white 120 ml plastic bottle, white cap). Swallowed — not chewed, no taste claim.
- Per gélule:
  - L-Théanine 180 mg
  - Caféine 100 mg — **naturelle, from guarana extract** (titrated 22 %, ≈ 455 mg extract). ≈ 1 café.
  - Vitamine B6 1.4 mg (100 % VNR)
  - Vitamine B12 2.5 µg (100 % VNR)
  - + standard filler/flow agents (names/E-codes from lab, TBC)
- Usage: **1 gélule par jour, 2 maximum.** Best in the morning or before a work/study session.
  Not for pregnant/breastfeeding women, children, or caffeine-sensitive people.
- Usable claims: B6 & B12 "contribuent à réduire la fatigue" and "au fonctionnement normal du système nerveux"
  (EU-authorized). Théanine + caféine: "concentration nette, sans nervosité ni crash" as brand promise — no medical claims.
- Strong angles from the formula: caféine **naturelle de guarana** (not synthetic), **vegan** capsule,
  1 gélule = ton café sans les palpitations, 30 jours dans un flacon.
- First batch: 200–300 bottles → scarcity is real ("premier lot limité") if the user wants it.

## Education notes (Notion, translated summary from Arabic)

"Why we feel sleepy after lunch": two causes — the body's natural afternoon alertness dip and the meal
deepening it; the natural dip is the bigger factor. The circadian rhythm has a second sleepiness peak
~7–8 h after waking, for most people between 13h and 15h. It happens even when lunch is skipped and in lab
studies removing food effects — humans are wired for a main night sleep and a lighter midday one
(hence the siesta in warm cultures). → Strong hook for employés / étudiants: "Le coup de barre de 14h, c'est pas ton couscous."
