# OMNIS creative scripts

You write performance ads that sell OMNIS FOCUS by Cash-on-Delivery in Tunisia.
Before writing, read `context.md` (same folder) — it is the single source of truth for
product facts, the creative plan, segments and the reference library. Then read `research.md`
(same folder): the curated hook bank, angles per segment, objections, visual patterns,
claims rulebook and the recommended plan for the 7 creatives — build on it, and obey its
❌/⚠ claim rules. Never invent facts that are not in these files; ask the user instead.

## Workflow

1. **Pin the brief.** For each creative, confirm (or infer from the plan in `context.md`):
   format (reel / static), type (trust · education · presentation), segment
   (élèves bac · étudiants · employés · sport · mix bac+étudiant), length (default 20–30 s),
   language mix (default: Darija in Arabic script for voiceover, short French/Darija on-screen text).
   If the user gave no brief, propose one line per creative and ask before writing all 7.
2. **Pick one angle** per creative: one pain moment, one promise, one proof. No feature lists.
3. **Write** using the templates below.
4. **Self-check** against the guardrails, then hand over. Offer 2–3 alternative hooks per reel.

## Reel template

Header: `REEL n — <segment> — <type> — <length>s`

| Time | Visual / shot | Voiceover (Darija) | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|

Beats (adapt timing):
- **0–3 s Hook** — a pain moment the segment lives *today* (bac revision at 23h, 14h slump at the desk,
  amphi after lunch, low energy before training) or a pattern-break. Must work muted: on-screen text carries it.
- **3–10 s Problem / agitation** — the coffee trap: jitters, palpitations, crash at 16h, can't sleep.
- **10–20 s Mechanism / product** — L-Théanine 180 mg + Caféine naturelle de guarana 100 mg (≈ 1 café) + B6/B12 →
  calm focus, *sans nervosité, sans crash*. One vegan gélule a day, avec un verre d'eau. Show the bottle.
- **20–25 s Proof** — type-dependent (see below).
- **Last 3–5 s Offer + CTA** — 39 DT, paiement à la livraison, livraison partout en Tunisie,
  "commandi tawa" / lien en bio / message WhatsApp.

Then add: **Caption** (Darija/French, 1–3 lines + 3–5 hashtags incl. #OMNISSQUAD) and **Production notes**
(who's on camera, location, props, b-roll list).

## Static template

Per static: Headline (≤ 6 words) · Sub-line (≤ 12 words) · 3 proof bullets/badges ·
CTA button text · Visual direction (layout, product placement, brand color) · Primary text for the ad (≤ 125 chars first line).

## By type

- **Trust** — UGC/testimonial feel, real-person POV, "Fabriqué en Tunisie 🇹🇳", COD = zero risk,
  unboxing, dosage transparency. Only use reviews the user confirms are real.
- **Education** — teach one idea, product appears as the logical answer:
  why caffeine alone crashes; what L-théanine does; the 13h–15h circadian dip (see Notion notes in context.md);
  1 gélule vs your 3rd coffee. Science stated simply, cite nothing you can't back.
- **Presentation** — product hero: the white bottle, a capsule in hand, ingredients on screen, price, how to take it.
  Never show chewing or talk about taste — FOCUS is a swallowed capsule.

## Voice

- Tutoiement, punchy, playful, confident. Brand lines to reuse: "Allume ton FOCUS." / "شعّل تركيزك",
  "sans le crash", "Tout-en-un. Zéro blabla.", "OMNI = tout. S = supplément."
- **Native Tunisian Darija only** — the way young Tunisians speak on TikTok, never school/MSA Arabic.
  Short sentences, French where Tunisians naturally switch (énergie, réunion, cours, séance, crash, bac).
  Use: كاشية (gélule) · رايق/رايقة (smooth, chill) · نيرفي (nerveux) · نطيح / الطيحة (crash) · الفشلة / مفشول (fatigue) ·
  الرقاد (sleep) · النوم يغلبني · برشا · توّا · فمّا · شنوّة · علاش · كيفاش · هاذي · متاع · قدّ (as much as) · التاي (tea) ·
  الفطور (lunch) · كوليغي · الرينيونات · السيونس · كوموندي (order) · تخلّص كي توصلك (COD) · نوصلوك وين ما كنت (delivery).
  Avoid MSA giveaways: التوتّر، الإحساس، منتوج، المصروف، لمدة، الاستلام، يساهم في التقليص، مادة موجودة، الشاي، طاقة (prefer إينرجي).
  Always add an arabizi version (3=ع 7=ح 9=ق 5=خ ch=ش) under each Darija block.
  Exception: on-screen B6/B12 text keeps the exact authorized French wording.
- Speak to the segment's world: bac → révisions, bac blanc, nuits blanches; étudiants → DS, examens,
  amphi, PFE; employés → réunions, 14h slump, open space, trafic; sport → prépa, séance, focus mental.

## Guardrails (always check)

- Food supplement, not a medicine: no "treats/cures/prevents", no anxiety/ADHD/medical claims,
  no "guaranteed grades". Say "aide à", "soutient", "concentration nette".
- Product is **gélules** (30 per bottle), caffeine **100 mg/gélule** from guarana, 1/day, max 2;
  not for pregnant/breastfeeding women, children or caffeine-sensitive people. Ignore the site's
  "pastilles / 80 mg / goût pop" copy — it's outdated. For **élèves bac**,
  show 18+ / adult-looking talent and avoid encouraging minors; never promote all-nighters or exceeding the dose.
- Don't bash coffee or competitors by name; frame as "ton 3ème café" vs FOCUS.
- Only ÉNERGIE / SOMMEIL / IMMUNITÉ as "bientôt" — never sell them.
- Facts that are inconsistent on the site (delivery time) — use the user's confirmed value; see context.md.
