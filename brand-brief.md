# OMNIS FOCUS — brand brief

_Single source of truth for the site, ads and scripts. Keep in sync with `.claude/skills/omnis-scripts/context.md`._

## The ONE promise
**L'énergie d'un café, sans le crash.** — Tunisian: « الإينرجي متاع قهوة، من غير الطيحة. »

## Primary CTA
**« Je commande · 39 DT »** → product page order form, cash on delivery.
Secondary (info): « Comment ça marche ».

## Ideal customers
- Students (bac 18+, university) during revisions and exams
- Office workers with long days, meetings and the 14h slump
- Gym-goers who want energy before training without an over-revved feeling

## Core benefits (outcomes — "so that…")
1. **Concentré, pas nerveux** — caffeine + L-théanine for a smoother energy, so you finish what you start.
2. **Des après-midi qui tiennent** — more regular energy than coffee alone, so 14h stops being a wall.
3. **Moins de fatigue** — B6 and B12 contribute to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue.
4. **Une routine de 5 secondes** — one capsule with water; nothing to prepare or buy every day.

## #1 proof (today)
Formula transparency: exact mg on the label and site, made by a Tunisian lab, pay only on delivery.
_Next: first real customer reviews (WhatsApp screenshots with permission), lab name + registration number._

## Top objections → answers
| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Caffeine every day, is it safe?" | 100 mg = one coffee; 1/day, max 2; not for children, pregnant/breastfeeding or caffeine-sensitive people. |
| "Is this a real brand / can I trust it?" | Tunisian lab, full formula in mg, and you pay only when you receive it. |
| "What if it arrives damaged?" | WhatsApp a photo within 48h → replacement bottle. |
| "Will it ruin my sleep?" | Take it like a coffee: morning or before a session, not in the evening. |
