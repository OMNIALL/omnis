## Preliminary positioning report (hypothesis — replace with the AI project output once sheets 2–4 are real)

**1. Market snapshot.** Energy in Tunisia is sold as a *drink* (coffee, energy drinks) or as *medicine* (pharmacy vitamins, ginseng). Drinks promise intensity and taste; pharmacy products promise "fatigue" relief with medical seriousness and no brand story. Instagram nootropics promise "brain power" with vague, imported formulas. Almost nobody talks about the *pattern* — the spike and the crash — or shows exact doses.

**2. Saturated angles (avoid).** "Plus d'énergie", "boost", "performance", "énergie naturelle" without a mechanism, discount-led "−30 %" pages, medical/fatigue claims.

**3. White space.** The steady day: *energy without the crash*, from a **transparent, Tunisian, COD** brand that speaks Darija and has a human on WhatsApp. Positioned **next to the first coffee, against the third one**.

**4. Recommended angle.** *"Ce n'est pas l'énergie qui te manque, c'est le crash de 14h."* — pain avoidance as the core desire, transparency + COD as the trust engine, students in exam season as the first niche. To be confirmed with real customer language (sheet 2).

**5. Headlines to test** (rewrite with the exact words from CIA interviews):
1. *L'énergie d'un café. Sans le crash de 14h.* (current — control)
2. *Arrête le troisième café. Garde la tête claire jusqu'au soir.*
3. *14h, tu es encore là. Une gélule le matin, payée à la livraison.*

**6. Risks.**
- Proof gap: zero real customers yet — any "ça marche" line without reviews lowers certainty.
- Claim risk: "focus/concentration" wording must stay within authorized claims; no grades, no stress.
- Caffeine-sensitive and under-18 audiences (students!) — targeting and copy must exclude minors.
- If COGS > 25 % of price, the value-stack add-ons and the guarantee may not be affordable at scale.
