# OMNIS FOCUS: 7 creatives (v2: native Tunisian)

_2 statics + 5 reels · Tunisian Darija voiceover (Arabic script + arabizi) + short FR on-screen text · 9:16 for reels, 1:1 and 4:5 for statics._
Built from the Notion plan, the curated Gemini research and the lab-confirmed formula.

**Product facts used everywhere:** 30 gélules végétales · 1 gélule/jour (max 2) · 100 mg caféine naturelle de guarana (≈ 1 café) ·
180 mg L-théanine · B6 + B12 · Fabriqué en Tunisie · 39 DT · paiement à la livraison · livraison partout en Tunisie.
Delivery time is deliberately **not** promised (24–48h vs 2–5 jours is still unconfirmed).

**How it's written:** street Tunisian, not Arabic-from-school. Words like _كاشية_ (gélule), _رايقة_ (smooth/chill),
_نيرفي_ (nerveux), _نطيح_ (crash), _الفشلة_ (fatigue), _الرقاد_ (sleep), _كوموندي_ (commande), _تخلّص كي توصلك_ (paiement à la livraison),
with French where Tunisians naturally switch (énergie, réunion, cours, séance, crash).

**Arabizi key:** 3 = ع · 7 = ح · 9 = ق · 5 = خ · gh = غ · ch = ش · ou = و · 2 = ء. Spelling is conversational, so creators can adjust it.
