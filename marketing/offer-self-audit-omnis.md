# Offer Self-Audit — OMNIS FOCUS (Part 2 of the worksheet)

**Brand / product:** OMNIS FOCUS — 30 gélules (L-théanine 180 mg, caféine naturelle de guarana 100 mg, B6, B12), 39 / 72 / 99 DT, COD.
**Offer link:** `/products/focus#order-form` · **Audit date:** 2026-11-15 (after Phase 0).
Same honesty rule as the Lunésia example: the score is only useful if the answers are.

### Q1 — What mass-market desire does my offer connect to? · DESIRE · **Medium**
Health / pain avoidance: the 14h crash and the jitters from coffee #3. Real, daily, felt by students and office workers.
Why not Strong: the site and ads still mix three signals (crash, focus, "énergie") and the status layer (being the one who delivers) isn't committed anywhere. Fix: headline = pain avoidance; status only in identity lines.

### Q2 — What is the transformation, before and after? · DESIRE · **Strong**
Before: first coffee 9h, third coffee 11h nervous, crash 14h (ProblemSection timeline). After: one capsule, energy that rises gently and doesn't collapse (EnergyCurve). Concrete and visual — most Tunisian brands skip this.

### Q3 — Perceived value vs price? · VALUE STACK · **Medium**
39 DT ≈ 1,30 DT/day, down to 1,10 DT/day in pack 3; guide + WhatsApp follow-up + free delivery from 2. Not yet 5–10× perceived value: the guide is invisible until after purchase and the strikethrough prices are discount anchors (the Lunésia trap). Fix: show the guide (cover + 3 chapters) in the buy box, lead with "routine", not "−6 DT".

### Q4 — Complete solution, not just a product? · VALUE STACK · **Medium**
Product + 30-day protocol + follow-up = a routine. Weak point: it's described in one line. Fix: a "Ta routine 30 jours" block (what you get on day 0 / 7 / 14 / 25), exam-session framing, optional sticker sheet.

### Q5 — Biggest risk a cold stranger feels, and how the offer removes it? · RISK & PROOF · **Medium**
Risk: "encore un complément qui ne fait rien, 39 DT perdus" + "est-ce une arnaque ?". Removed by COD (no payment before seeing the product), courier calls first, sealed-bottle guarantee. Why not Strong: the guarantee sits in the buy box and FAQ, not in the hero or ads — it's framed as policy, not as confidence.

### Q6 — What proof do I have that the transformation is real? · RISK & PROOF · **Weak**
9 PubMed studies + Examine.com on caffeine + L-théanine, exact label, Tunisian-made. **Zero real customers, faces or reviews yet.** In a supplement-skeptical market this is the biggest gap. Fix: tester programme → doubt-first stories, real first names + city, "produit offert"; founder face; lab certificate.

### Q7 — Would a stranger understand the value in 5 seconds? · DESIRE + VALUE · **Strong**
"L'énergie d'un café. Sans le crash." answers *why this over coffee* immediately, with the bottle and the price above the fold.

**Scoring rule:** under 4 Strong → **fix the offer before touching the ads.**
**Weakest lever:** **Risk & Proof** (Q6 Weak, Q5 Medium).
**Single change this week:** ship the 30 tester bottles and run the 10 CIA interviews; in parallel move the guarantee line next to the hero CTA.

**Target after Phase 1–2:** Q1, Q5, Q6 → Strong (5 Strong) = "refine, then scale".
