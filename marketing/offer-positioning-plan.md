**2 Strong / 4 Medium / 1 Weak → "under 4 Strong: fix the offer before touching the ads."**
**Weakest lever: Risk & Proof** (no real customers yet). That decides the order of everything below.

### The three thresholds (cold Tunisian visitor, today)
| Threshold | Where it is | What moves it for OMNIS |
|---|---|---|
| **Desire vs cost** (price, effort, time, sacrifice) | Close: the 14h crash is felt daily, 39 DT is low, COD = no effort | Identity framing, daily cost (1,10 DT/day), guide + follow-up |
| **Certainty** ("will it work for me?") | **Low** — supplement skepticism is extreme in Tunisia | Mechanism reframe, studies, *doubt-first* testimonials from people like them, demonstration (UGC) |
| **Trust** (in the brand) | **Low** — new brand, no faces | Founder face & story, real reviews, lab certificate, Tunisian-made, WhatsApp human, COD |

### Market awareness & sophistication
- **Awareness:** most of the market is **problem-aware and passive** — they live the crash, they don't search for a solution, their "solution" is more coffee. → **Interrupt funnel**: social ad → story/education page → intro offer (funnel matrix).
- **Sophistication:** "energy" is **stage 5** (coffee, Red Bull, energy drinks — everyone's bored and skeptical). "Focus supplement with exact doses" is **stage 1–2 in Tunisia**. → Don't shout "more energy" (losing zone). Lead with a **mechanism** (stage-3 move): *"Ce n'est pas l'énergie qui te manque. C'est le pic, puis la chute."* Caffeine + L-théanine = the energy without the spike-and-crash pattern of the third coffee.

### Desire (four universal desires + Life Force 8)
- **Primary: Health / pain avoidance** — LF8 #3 "freedom from fear, pain and danger": the crash, the jitters, the wasted afternoon.
- **Secondary: Status** — LF8 #6/#8: being the one who delivers, finishes, holds the meeting. Used as *identity*, never as a promise of grades or results.
- **Lunésia lesson:** don't split the signal. Headline = pain avoidance; status lives in the moments/identity lines and creatives.

## 6. Validation — PMF worksheet (`pmf-validation-omnis.md`)

**Minimum viable test (before reordering stock / scaling):** the current site as MV landing page · 2–3 creatives from the best CIA angle · small broad Meta budget.

| Metric | Target before launch | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **COGS** | **≤ 25 % of selling price** → ≤ 9,75 DT per bottle at 39 DT | If higher, the price/pack structure must change before scaling |
| **CVR** (product page, cold) | **≥ 4 %** | Low CVR = offer/message problem, not traffic |
| **Max CPA** | AOV − COGS − delivery cost − packaging − COD refusal loss − target profit | Formula + worked example in the PMF file; fill with your real unit cost |
| **Delivery acceptance** | ≥ 80 % of shipped COD orders accepted | Below → WhatsApp confirmation and trust work first |
| **Engagement** | Comments/DMs/shares on ads | Strong engagement + weak CVR = angle right, offer/price wrong |
| **Test budget** | ≈ 170–250 $ at 20–30 $/day (course benchmark) | Enough for signal, not enough to burn cash |

**Go / no-go:** targets hit → scale + build the brand. Missed → diagnose the weakest threshold → change **one** N.T.P.M.A variable → retest, or kill the angle.

**Warning signals after launch:** rising CPA with no creative change (angle fatigue) · high CVR but low repeat (product not delivering the promise) · strong engagement, weak conversion (price/offer) · low delivery acceptance (trust).

## 7. Creative system

- **Angle first:** the main reasons someone converts = (1) no 14h crash, (2) no jitters from coffee #3, (3) exam/work days that count, (4) transparent Tunisian formula, (5) pay on delivery.
- **Formats:** image · video · carousel. **Secondary formats to rotate** (format tree): UGC with voice-over, us vs them (FOCUS vs 3 cafés), before/after day timeline, founder, text-message chain (real WhatsApp screenshots only), post-it notes, whiteboard mechanism, statistics (exact mg), bullet points.
- **Minimum viable creative set:** 3 UGC with voice-over + 3 static concepts (course case study), Darija-first with the `omnis-scripts` skill.
- **Every asset goes through:** draft (2–3 variations, baton handoff between elements) → **self-review checklist** (lizard-brain scan, baton handoff, threshold check, market language, final pass) → **CIA insider review** next to a competitor's ad → launch only when the insider says "I'd choose this".

### Phase 3 — iterate one variable at a time (ongoing)
| Variable | First test |
|---|---|
| **Niche** | Students vs young professionals — which converts cheaper? |
| **Transformation** | "Sans le crash" vs "La tête claire jusqu'au soir" (hero A/B test wired) |

**Weekly review** (`weekly-review.md` + LPM iteration prompts): (a) which section hurts conversion, (b) hero A/B result and sample size, (c) add a proof/results section when reviews exist, (d) CVR drop diagnosis — plus the PMF warning signals.
