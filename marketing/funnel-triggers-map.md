# OMNIS funnel — triggers & effects map (Portland method)

Structure mined from `portland-funnel-map.pdf` and the chain of steps & triggers. We don't copy their words; we fill the proven structure with OMNIS research language (replace the placeholders with CIA quotes).
Each element: **what they see → trigger mechanism → mental step / effect → threshold movement** (desire, certainty, trust).
Persona for this map: **Yasmine, 21, L3 à Tunis, révisions de janvier**, three coffees a day, KO at 14h. (Second map later for the young-pro persona.)

## The chain at a glance
Attention → "c'est moi" (desire) → "c'est quoi ?" (certainty) → "ok je regarde" → "bon endroit" → "ils comprennent le schéma" → **"il y a une vraie raison"** (certainty ↑↑) → "quelqu'un comme moi" (trust) → "je sais à quoi m'attendre" (certainty crosses) → "je ne risque rien" → order → "exactement ce qu'ils avaient promis".
