# Programme testeurs — first 30 real reviews

Goal: 30 honest reviews (text + photo, some short videos) within ~3 weeks, so cold traffic sees real people before we scale ads.

## Who (30 bottles)
| Segment | How many | Where to find them |
|---|---|---|
| Students (bac, fac, prépa) | 12 | Your network, student Facebook groups, campus ambassadors |
| Young professionals | 8 | LinkedIn / friends' offices, coworking spaces |
| Gym-goers | 5 | 1–2 local gyms (ask the coach) |
| Gamers / streamers | 5 | Local Discord / TikTok micro-creators |

**Exclude:** under 18, pregnant/breastfeeding, caffeine-sensitive (ask before sending: same list as the label).

## The deal (say it exactly like this)
> Darija: نبعثولك علبة FOCUS ببلاش. تستعملها 14 يوم، و بعد تعطينا رايك الصحيح، مزيان ولا موش مزيان، مع تصويرة. كان نحطّوه على السيت، نكتبو « منتوج هدية ».
> FR : On t'offre un flacon de FOCUS. Tu l'utilises 14 jours, puis tu nous donnes ton avis honnête, positif ou non, avec une photo. Si on l'affiche, on précise « produit offert ».

## Timeline
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 | Send bottle + guide (WhatsApp day-0 message) |
| 7 | Check-in message |
| 14 | Review request: note /5, 2–3 sentences (what changed in their day), photo; video optional |
| 15–21 | Collect, ask display permission, log in the Sheet |

## What to ask (prompts, not scripts)
- Comment tu prenais ton café avant ?
- Dans quelle situation tu l'as prise (révision, boulot, sport, gaming) ?
- Qu'est-ce qui était différent dans ta journée ?
- Quelque chose que tu n'as pas aimé ?

## Rules (CLAUDE.md)
- Never write or edit a review for someone. Never pay for positive reviews only.
- Show every displayed tester review with **« produit offert »**.
- Reviews claiming medical effects (stress, anxiety, ADHD…) are **not displayed**, even if sincere.
- Site switches from trust cards to real reviews when ≥ 10 are in (`SocialProof.tsx`).

## Cost
30 bottles × unit cost + delivery. Replaces paying for ads that convert badly without proof.
